Our mission

The place we wish we'd had when we were starting college.

We built AstroPro around a simple regret: we wish we'd known everything that was out there when we were applying to college — the scholarships we never heard about, the financial aid rules nobody explained, the off-campus apartment search with no idea where to even start. So we put it all in one place, matched to you, so starting college doesn't mean figuring it out alone. That's AstroPro. For you.

Always free to students, always your choice what you hear about, and we never sell your information to anyone.
