THE LOGO
The Step is three connected blocks rising to the right: a small start becomes forward motion.
Use src-lockup-primary.svg on light backgrounds and src-lockup-reverse.svg on dark backgrounds.
The mono and white lockups are for single-color use. src-wordmark-* is the SRC shorthand.
SVG files contain outlined lettering, so they do not require fonts to render correctly.
Transparent PNGs are supplied at 8x the SVG master size.
Keep at least one step width clear around the logo. Use the full lockup at 180px wide or larger. Use the symbol below that, at least 20px wide.
Keep the original proportions. Do not rotate, stretch, outline, add shadows, or recolor outside the provided palette.

COLOR
Move Blue  #2448F2
Signal Lime #DAFF3E
Ink        #17191B
Paper      #F7F7F2
Body text  #5E626B
Use white text on blue, ink text on lime, and ink text on paper. Lime is an accent, not small text on white.

TYPOGRAPHY
Headlines: Sora, weights 700–800. Short headlines, tight spacing, sentence case in the interface.
Body and interface: Work Sans, weights 400–650. Keep small interface text at least 12px and phone inputs at least 16px.
Font license notices are included. Font files are supplied in fonts/.

VOICE
Encouraging, direct, practical. Respect students' agency.
Lead with clear actions: Find your scholarship. Build experience. Find your place.
Be specific about the service: Free to explore. No account needed.
Avoid invented urgency, guaranteed awards, and promises of admission, employment, or housing.
Keep “A project by AstroPro” small and separate from the main logo.
