ASTROPRO STUDENT RESOURCE CENTER
Student-edition identity and usage guide

The AstroPro name, star, and ringed planet remain the visual foundation. The student edition adds a graduation cap and the descriptor STUDENT RESOURCE CENTER. This is a project-specific AstroPro identity.

LOGO USE
Use the full logo at 190px wide or larger on a light, uncluttered background. Give it clear space at least the height of its student descriptor. Use the planet symbol at small sizes, at least 24px wide. Keep the original proportions and colors. Avoid stretching, rotation, extra effects, and placing the navy student descriptor on a dark background. The full logo is a raster asset; the separate small symbol is SVG.

Use navy on ivory, white on navy, and the darker Text Accent for small orange-toned text. Bright Astro Orange is an accent rather than small text on white.

TYPOGRAPHY
Sora 550–600 for headings. Work Sans 400–600 for reading and controls. Short headings, clear actions, generous spacing. Keep mobile inputs at 16px and supporting interface labels around 12px or larger.

VOICE
Warm, straightforward, practical. Help students find a useful starting point. Keep expectations honest: no guaranteed scholarship, admission, job, rental, or response-time claims.

ARTWORK
The custom student logo was created with the built-in image_gen tool using AstroPro's existing master logo as the reference/edit target. The campus photo is generated editorial imagery and does not represent a named school, an actual partnership, or a testimonial. No photo subject is identified as an AstroPro customer.
