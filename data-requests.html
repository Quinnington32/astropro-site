Data requests

Want to see what information we have about you, fix something that's wrong, or have your data deleted? Here's exactly how.

What you can ask for

  • See it. Ask what information we have on file connected to your email address.
  • Correct it. If something's wrong (a typo in your school, an outdated email, etc.), tell us and we'll fix it.
  • Delete it. Ask us to remove your information entirely from our systems and mailing list.

This applies whether you're a student whose school shared your email with us, or anyone else who's submitted information to this site.

How to make a request right now

We're a small, growing team and don't have an automated self-service tool built for this yet — so the real, working way to make a data request today is to email us directly. A person reads and handles every one of these.

Email a data request →

Tip: let us know which email address to look up, and whether you want to see, correct, or delete your information.

Response time: We aim to respond within a few business days. We're still a small team, so thank you for your patience — we read every message.
