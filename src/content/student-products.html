Checklist

Dorm and apartment essentials

Sleep

Bedding sized for your mattress (many dorms use Twin XL), a pillow, a mattress topper if allowed, and a small fan.

Study

A desk lamp, power strip with surge protection, headphones, backpack, and a water bottle.

Bath and laundry

Towels, shower caddy, shower shoes, laundry bag or basket, and detergent.

Kitchen

If you have a kitchen: a pot, pan, basic utensils, and a few dishes. Ask roommates before duplicating anything.

Check the rules first

Schools often ban some items such as candles, certain heaters, and extension cords. Read your housing’s allowed and prohibited list before you buy.

Coordinate with roommates

Split shared items like a mini fridge, microwave, or rug so you each buy less.

School supplies

Notebooks, calculators, and gear

Wait for the syllabus

Don’t buy a graphing calculator, lab coat, or special notebooks until your instructor says what’s required. Many courses allow Desmos or a basic calculator.

Buy basics in bulk after back-to-school

Pens, paper, and folders go on deep clearance a few weeks into the term. Stock up then for the next term.

Ask your campus

Some schools run free supply closets, loaner laptops, or calculator loans through the library or student services. Find your school’s resources →

Use the tax credit

Required course supplies can count toward the American Opportunity Credit. Rules and limits apply. See the IRS guide →
