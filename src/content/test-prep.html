Study smarter

Make your prep count

Start with a real practice test

Take one official, timed practice test first so you know where you stand and what to study.

Use official materials

Practice questions from the test makers look the most like the real exam. Use them before commercial prep.

Review your mistakes

Go back through every missed question and write down why you missed it. That is where the points come from.

Use your library card

Many public and college libraries lend prep books and offer free online test-prep databases.

Be careful with paid courses

Check what a course includes and its refund policy before you pay. Free official prep is often enough to start.
