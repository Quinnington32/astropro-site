Real mentorship options you can use right now

Our own matching isn't live yet, so here's where to actually find a mentor today — free and paid.

Free

Your school's alumni or mentorship office

Most colleges run a formal alumni mentorship program through career services or a dedicated alumni relations office — often the fastest way to a mentor who already knows your school.

Check your school's alumni office
Free

LinkedIn's alumni tool

Search LinkedIn for your school's alumni page, then use the built-in alumni tool to filter by field, employer or location — a direct way to find and message people a few years ahead of you.

Visit LinkedIn →
Free

ADPList

A large, free mentorship platform, strongest in tech, design and product, where you can book time with real working professionals at no cost.

Visit ADPList →
Paid

MentorCruise

A paid 1-on-1 mentorship marketplace with mentors from companies like Amazon, Airbnb and Netflix, across tech, business and more — useful if you want a more structured, ongoing relationship.
