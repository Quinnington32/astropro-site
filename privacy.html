Privacy

Privacy policy

A plain-language explanation of what information we collect, why we collect it, and what we do (and don't do) with it.

Honest note: We're a small, still-growing company. This page is a plain-language summary written by our team, not a polished legal document reviewed by a lawyer. We're working on a full legal privacy policy and will post it here once it's finalized — before this site is handling real user data at any real scale. If anything below is unclear or you want more detail, just email us.

What information we collect

There are two main ways we come to have your information:

  • School-provided student email addresses. We partner with schools, and in that partnership a school may give us a list of student email addresses so we can reach students with relevant scholarships, jobs, housing, and resources. We don't buy email lists from data brokers and we don't scrape them from the internet.
  • Whatever you submit yourself. If you fill out a form on this site — for example to submit an opportunity as an organization, or to reach out to us — we collect whatever you typed into that form (name, email, organization, message, etc.).

We don't collect sensitive categories of information (like health, financial account numbers, or government ID numbers) through this site, and we ask that you not send us anything like that either.

How we use your information

We use student email addresses to send emails connecting students with scholarships, internships, jobs, off-campus housing listings, and other resources that are relevant to them. That's the whole purpose of the list — helping students find real opportunities, not general marketing.

We use information organizations submit through our listing form to review, feature, and (where relevant) forward their opportunity to students on the network.

We don't sell your email address

We do not sell or hand over the student email list, in bulk or individually, to property managers, scholarship sponsors, employers, or any other third party. Ever.

Here's how it actually works when an organization wants to reach students: they submit their opportunity (a scholarship, a job listing, a property, etc.) to us through our site. If we feature it, we either list it in the Resource Center or include it in an email we send out ourselves to relevant students. The organization gets their opportunity in front of students — they do not get a copy of the student email list itself. We stay the only ones who hold and send to that list.

Unsubscribing and your choices

You can unsubscribe at any time. Every email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe link right in the message, no account or login required. You can also visit our Unsubscribe page for an immediate alternative.

If you'd like to see what information we have about you, correct it, or have it deleted entirely, visit our Data Requests page or just email us directly — see below.

Questions about your data or this policy?

Reach out any time and a real person on our team will get back to you.
